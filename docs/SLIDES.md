# Stakeholder deck

The five-slide business presentation is generated by `uv run python scripts/build_slides.py`:
`deliverables/london-office-market-agent.pptx`, with a PDF export alongside it.

1. Fragmented, repetitive market research and its effect on meeting preparation.
2. Monitor, Investigate and Prepare: questions, evidence and business outputs.
3. City vs West End: test growth, demand, supply and counterevidence before choosing a verdict.
4. Trust: source values, deterministic calculations, disagreement and uncertainty.
5. PoC to automated monitoring, alerts and private data, after workflow validation.

The investigation slide illustrates the decision process; it does not assert a current market
verdict. The deck makes no unmeasured time-saving claim. Measure time to a usable brief and time
to verify claims against the team's manual baseline. Implementation details and old infrastructure
blockers have been removed from the business storyline. Regenerating slides does not run live
acceptance checks; see the browser acceptance record for actual verification.
